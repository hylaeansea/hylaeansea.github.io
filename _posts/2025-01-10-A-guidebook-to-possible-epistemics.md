# Thesis

In the first part of this adolescent century we have seen unprescidented technological, cultural, and political change across nearly every corner of human society. Yet with this change, and because of it, we are also running headlong into deep ecological, cultural, economic, and social fissures with traditional forms of communal problem solving and epistemic discovery breaking down. Technology generally, and artificial intelligence more specifically, is at the epicenter of many of these fissures, and so we should embark on a project of ensuring that we understand this new fire from the gods and certainly erect logical guards, alarms, and mitigations against even the most wild predictions of our intellect. 

But also, in our short experience with it, we have developed some heuristics, ideas, and guides about how to leverage it not to supplant human creativity, economic output, or agency, but to endow everyone (and I do mean everyone) with cognitive superpowers, link dispperate, but interrelated disciplines, and dramatically expand communication and cooperative decision making. 

The core of this idea is a triad of models; The Mental Models that all of us carry around in our minds, that form our mirror of the world around us, that we are continuously updating and refining to be a more perfect match for the physical universe; The World Models that are most commonly computational, but can be scale physical models, engineering prototypes, even policy prototypes, but by their very nature are a simulation of the full system, an often digital facsimile that allows us to play forward simulations, ask what if questions, and get at the critical heart of the theory or theories we are exploring; and Language Models, the ubiquitous computational tools that underpin much of technological and economic development today, the basket in which we are placing all of our political and economic "eggs", but also a near-infinitely scalable tool that models one of the most fundamental features of human beings, our language, in all its forms. 

Each of these classes of models has been used to make great progress in nearly every human creative endeavour, but when combined, with these three interlocking wheels forming interconnected systems of knowledge store, ideation, physics grounding, translation, and most crucially, extremely fast iteration, we can create ecosystems of epistemic exploration that allow the three classes of models to be used for their individual strenghts, while leaning on the others in their areas of weakness. The ecology of ideas, with comprehensible interfaces of communication, break open the large, monolithic black boxes that we struggle to understand, and provide checkpoints, new insights, and new ways of exploring language, ideas, simulation, and knowledge and forming what Eric Winsberg terms *"A Model of the Phenomena."*[^1]

This concept is a nascent one, and maybe one that isn't particularly novel, but I think with some concrete framing, with some real world examples, and with some energetic vision casting, we can collectively build these systems, move ideas, knowledge, and wisdom through their myriad of feedback loops, and create intellectual common ground for the solving of problems yes, but more excitingly, the exploration of what is knowable, and what is possible.
