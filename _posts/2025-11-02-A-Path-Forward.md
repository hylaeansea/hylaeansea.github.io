# A Path Forward

This is an experiment in the form of a stream of though concerning how we can move forward as a society, and leverage the as-yet unfathomable power of large language models, record setting amounts of investment, to impact the abundance of public policy, collective knowledge, education, bio-diversity, and epistemological problems that we face. 

I am not sure where this will lead, as this is only the first couple of paragraphs of this idea, from which I might wander down winding paths of ideas, problems, opinions and mythologies, but we have to start somewhere, and we know we can't continue down this path that we are on now.

This isn't even a primarily political essay, though its ideas, its calls to action, its motives have been sharpened and brought to focus by the current divisive political climate in the west, and especially in the US. Though I will try to steer clear of taking political sides, I feel that some of my bias is liable to bleed through these words, and because of this I am especially focused on trying to argue from first principles, from basic inalienable truths about the human condition that every person would agree to and base an argument on. 

With that as a primer, lets get started -

## AI - Fire From The Gods

In its current state, it can provide us with powers we cannot yet comprehend - the ability to affect every aspect of society will come as the future gets more evenly distributed, but we still have agency and can make decisions, policies, and ideas to have it work for and with humanity, rather than against and independent of it - but we have to act now - we have to get as many people fluent in the language of AI, chatbots, agentic systems, etc as possible - 

This is easier to do that say for quantum computing, or detailed public policy, because it is built on Natural Language and the thing about natural language is that it is natural - 

The only way this works is if we can scatter this manna of knowledge and silicon as far as possible into all the socio-economic, geographic, and idealogical layers as possible. In short, everyone who can use human language, can use this, and a foundational lemma is:

The more people that engage, the more aligned, diverse, fair, and productive it will be,

This is the network effects of the Renessaince City, and Roman Road Network, and University System, and Western Capitilist Economic System - but unfortunately it is also the network effects of the social media isolation, echo chambers, Rohinga genocide - economic cartels, and exploitative practices of colonialism - 

But the very nature of the tech, is that it gives us insight into these patterns, we cant understand how facebooks algorithms work or how it creates a walled garden, but with a diversity of AI companies, labs, researchers, regulators, and users, we can form a collective understanding of this technology as we grow with it, as we evolve it - we can have it write sensors and dashboards and papers, and help us form mental models about what it is and what it is doing
